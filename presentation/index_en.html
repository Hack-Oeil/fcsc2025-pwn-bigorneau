Bigorneau

We often try to obtain shellcodes with weird constraints, either in size, alphanumeric, etc. Here, we try a new weird constraint for an x64 shellcode: you are only allowed to use 6 distinct byte values (i.e., len(set(shellcode)) <= 6)!
